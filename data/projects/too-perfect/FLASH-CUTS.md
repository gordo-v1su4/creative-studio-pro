# Too Perfect: flash cuts and misdirects (draft, 2026-11-02)

The trailer lies on purpose: it cuts together moments that don't belong together, so you think one thing happened, and the pilot shows it was something else.

**Cast:**
- **Rafa Quiñones:** the lead. His blood is what the squad doses on.

**How the dose works (keep it clinical, never a mouth on a wound):** Rafa gives blood like a donor: a tourniquet, a needle in the crook of the elbow, a bag filling on the floor. The squad takes it like an IV hydration session: a drip line into the back of the hand or the inner arm, the bag hung from a locker door or an IV pole in the trainer's room, everyone in a row of folding chairs, scrolling their phones like it's recovery. To anyone walking in, it looks like vitamin drips.
- **Tess Lindqvist:** the flyer who's breaking.
- **Marcus Hale:** the college president's son, and Rafa's secret love interest.
- **Coach Lorraine Duvall:** knows everything.

**Locations:**
- The Cardinals gym: `locations/gym-*`
- The locker room: `locations/locker-*`

**References:**
- ***Euphoria*:** a voice-over that lies, and memory cuts mid-sentence.
- ***Breaking Bad* pilot:** open on the disaster, then "three weeks earlier".
- Trailer misdirection: cut a reaction onto the wrong cause.

## The misdirects (what the trailer makes you think vs what's true)

| The trailer shows | You think | The pilot shows |
|---|---|---|
| Tess collapses on the mat, the coach screaming, an ambulance light on the gym wall | Tess overdosed | Tess's body rejects the dose; it's the first sign the blood is wearing off |
| Marcus at a party, arm around a girl, laughing | Marcus is straight, the popular guy | The girl is his cover. Later a car picks Marcus up after practice; the driver is Rafa, and they kiss in the dark |
| Rafa's hands on Tess's throat in the locker room | Rafa is hurting Tess | Rafa is holding Tess's head up while she seizes |
| The coach burning a box of paper in a barrel behind the gym | The coach is covering up a crime | The coach is burning old Nationals test results that would show the squad has been "clean" since 1998: the problem is older than Rafa |
| Marcus on a pay phone: "Dad, it's him." | Marcus is turning Rafa in | Marcus is warning his father off: "It's him. Leave him alone." |
| A body bag on a gurney in a hallway | Someone on the squad died | The 1998 team. A photo on the coach's office wall: same uniforms, same faces, same ages, 2001 |

The last one is the real twist hook: the squad doesn't age. The trailer never says it; a viewer has to catch it on a second watch.

## Flash fragments (each half a second to a second, cut against the main shots)

Every action fragment has motion blur. Every gym fragment is on the blue spring floor. The look is anamorphic, dark and grainy.

**Body:**
1. Medical tape ripping off the crook of Rafa's elbow, a cotton ball, a pinprick of red. Close.
2. A drop of blood on the blue carpet, spreading into the weave.
3. Tess's nose bleeding at the top of a stunt, one line of red.
4. A rubber tourniquet snapping tight around an arm, a thumb tapping for the vein. You can't see whose.
5. A dark red bag on an IV pole, the drip chamber ticking, the line running down into Tess's hand, out of focus.
6. Tess's pupils blowing wide, extreme close-up.
7. A tooth on the mat.

**The squad:**

8. From the rafters: a two-high pyramid, and one base at the bottom (Marcus) buckles a knee on purpose. The stunt folds, the flyers are caught clean, nobody is hurt. Coach Lorraine: "Worse. Do it worse." The squad is hiding how good it is, one planned mistake at a time.
9. A basket toss so high the flyer disappears into the dark above the lights.
10. Chalk exploding off hands, slow, on the clap.
11. The squad in a circle, holding hands, eyes closed. A ritual or a prayer, you can't tell which.

**Marcus:**

12. Marcus watching from the bleachers, the only one not cheering.
13. Headlights in the gym parking lot, the engine running, a car door opening.
14. Two hands on a gearshift, one over the other. That's the only hint.
15. Marcus laughing at a party with the girl, then the same face alone in the car mirror, not laughing.
16. The president's office: a framed 1998 squad photo turned face-down on the desk.

**Night, memory and rumour:**

17. A Polaroid flash in the dark locker room.
18. The ice-pack cart rolling slowly on its own across the empty gym.
19. The coach's whistle dropped on the tile, still swinging on its lanyard.
20. Rafa on the gym roof at night, sitting on the edge, legs hanging.
21. Phones lighting up in a dark dorm hallway, one after another, all with the same message.
22. A pay phone receiver swinging on its cord.
23. A gurney wheel, fast, down a corridor.

**The turn (last 3 seconds, before the title):**

24. The 1998 photo on the coach's wall, pushing in on one face: Rafa, same age, same scar.
25. Hard cut to black. The sound of one drop hitting the floor.

## Lines for the dropouts (audio only, under black or a fragment)

- **Coach Lorraine:** *"Worse. Do it worse."*
- **Tess, laughing then not:** *"I can't feel my hands."*
- **Marcus, to his father:** *"It's him."*
- **Rafa, close, a whisper:** *"Nobody gets this good by practising."*
- **Voice-over, Tess, Euphoria-style:** *"Everyone thinks it's the cheer. It's never the cheer."*
