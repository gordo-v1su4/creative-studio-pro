## Act 2

### 050 PRT — The Party
Story beat: **Set-Up** (the crew passes; Mara notices).

**Action**
1. An off-campus house party: string lights over the backyard, bass through the walls, red cups everywhere.
2. Kai holds court by the keg; Malik, Elias and Rowan work the edges of the room.
3. Mara Voss, cup untouched, watches Lucian slip out the back door alone.
4. She follows him past the string lights into the dark end of the yard, by the fence.
5. A shape moves too fast between two trees; a party guest sags against the fence, Lucian's hand at their neck.
6. Lucian lifts his head and looks straight at Mara.
7. The guest blinks, laughs, wanders back toward the house as if nothing happened.

**Dialogue**
```
KAI (to the crowd, cup raised): "To the class of nobody-cares!"
MARA (to herself, setting her cup down): "Where are you going?"
PARTY GUEST (dazed, smiling): "Whoa. Head rush."
LUCIAN (quiet, wiping his thumb): "You should be inside."
```

### 060 EXP — Exposure Protocol
Story beat: **Set-Up** (the crew's playbook, and the crack in it).

**Action**
1. The kitchen, crowded; Mara pushes through to Kai at the counter.
2. Kai laughs her off, easy and warm, and hands her a fresh cup.
3. Elias steps in and, loud enough for the room, says she's had too much.
4. In the hallway, Malachi blocks her way out without touching her.
5. Rowan starts to say something to Mara; Elias cuts him off in front of everyone, and Rowan goes quiet.
6. On the porch, Malachi and Kai alone, the party behind the glass.

**Dialogue**
```
MARA (low, urgent): "Your friend just bit someone in the yard."
KAI (smiling, handing her a cup): "Lucian? He can barely bite a sandwich."
ELIAS (to the room, flat): "She's been drinking since nine."
ROWAN (stepping forward): "Mara, it's not—"
ELIAS (not looking at him): "Rowan. Kitchen."
MALACHI (on the porch, to Kai): "Step four?"
KAI (watching Mara through the glass): "Not yet."
```

### 070 MSN — Mara's Notebook
Story beat: **B Story** (Mara's notes).

**Action**
1. Mara's dorm room, past midnight; her roommate asleep under a blanket.
2. Mara pulls a notebook from under her mattress: weeks of dates, times, names.
3. Pages of observations: no one eats, no one is out at noon, the same five heads turning.
4. A sketch of the five heads turning at once, from the lecture hall.
5. She writes one word on a fresh page and underlines it twice.
6. She closes the notebook, puts on her jacket and leaves without waking her roommate.

**Dialogue**
```
MARA (whispering, reading her own notes): "Never at lunch. Never in the sun."
MARA (under her breath, pen stopping): "Okay."
```

### 080 WLK — The Walk
Story beat: **Set-Up** (the walk to the store; Malachi keeps pace).

**Action**
1. Veridian Avenue at closing time, empty, wet asphalt under the streetlights.
2. Mara walks fast, notebook against her chest.
3. The Video Haven sign ahead; its V flickers on and off.
4. Behind her, on a rooftop, a figure keeps pace with her steps.
5. She stops and turns; the roofline is empty.
6. She looks at the store's lit window and walks in.

**Dialogue**
```
MARA (to herself, eyes on the sign): "Just ask him."
```

## Act 3

### 090 CLT — Closing Time
Story beat: **Catalyst** (Mara says it out loud).

**Action**
1. Video Haven, near closing; Kai alone at the counter, counting the till.
2. The bell over the door; Mara walks in and sets the notebook on the counter.
3. Kai turns on the charm; she doesn't laugh.
4. She opens the notebook to the underlined word and turns it toward him.
5. Kai's face goes still.
6. He walks past her, flips the sign to CLOSED and locks the door.

**Dialogue**
```
KAI (easy, not looking up): "We close in five. Unless you want The Lost Boys."
MARA (setting the notebook down): "I want you to read this."
KAI (smiling, flipping a page): "You draw better than you take notes."
MARA (steady): "Say it's wrong."
KAI (after a long beat, quiet): "Go home, Mara."
MARA (not moving): "You don't eat. You don't burn. You don't get older."
```

### 100 STF — Step Four
Story beat: **Catalyst** (Kai's choice).

**Action**
1. The back room: boxes of tapes, a single bulb, the service door.
2. Kai pulls Mara back there, away from the window.
3. The service door opens; Malachi steps in and shuts it behind him.
4. Kai talks, fast and reasonable; Malachi doesn't answer.
5. Kai tells Mara to run; she doesn't move.
6. Malachi moves; Kai is suddenly between them.
7. Cut to black on Kai's face, deciding.

**Dialogue**
```
MALACHI (calm, closing the door): "Step four, Kai."
KAI (hands up, reasonable): "She's a freshman with a notebook. Nobody reads notebooks."
MALACHI (flat): "I read it."
KAI (to Mara, not taking his eyes off Malachi): "Run."
MARA (shaking, staying put): "No."
```

### 110 INS — 2:47 AM (From Inside)
Story beat: **Catalyst** (the opening replayed with what we now know).

**Action**
1. 2:47 AM; the back room; Mara wakes on the floor with black tears on her face.
2. A drunk customer bangs on the front glass.
3. Mara and Kai both go instantly normal: she wipes her face, he walks out front.
4. Kai waves the customer off through the glass with a smile.
5. Alone at the counter, Kai picks up the ringing phone, listens, hangs up.
6. He takes a red vial from his pocket and drinks it.

**Dialogue**
```
CUSTOMER (slurring, at the glass): "You're open! Sign says you're open!"
KAI (through the glass, smiling): "Sign's broken, man."
MARA (from the back, hoarse): "Kai? What did you do?"
```

### 120 ESC — The Escape
Story beat: **Catalyst** (the crew closes ranks).

**Action**
1. The rear door; Malik, Elias and Rowan are already there in the alley.
2. Kai carries Mara out and up the fire escape.
3. On the roof, the red vial passes from Malik to Elias to Rowan to Kai.
4. Malachi steps out of the rear door below and looks up.
5. Kai, Mara in his arms, leaps the gap to the next roof; the crew follows, one by one.
6. Wide on the rooftops at night: the crew running, the Video Haven V flickering behind them.

**Dialogue**
```
MALIK (handing off the vial, quick): "Keep it moving."
ROWAN (looking down at Malachi): "He saw."
ELIAS (already running): "Then don't stop."
KAI (to Mara, breathless): "Hold on."
```
