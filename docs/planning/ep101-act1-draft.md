## Act 1 — scene by scene

Names are exact reference names. Dialogue is one line each: `SPEAKER (delivery): "line"`.

### 020 MRQ — Morning Quad
Story beat: **Set-Up** (the crew passes).

**Action**
1. Morning sun on the college quad; orientation tables under the palms; freshmen in lanyards.
2. Kai "Hoodie" Santana crosses the lawn and heads turn; a sophomore asks him to sign a football.
3. Kai signs it and slides past the orientation signup sheet without writing his name.
4. Malik, Elias and Rowan fall into step around Kai, a loose orbit that looks like nothing.
5. Rowan squints at the sun; Elias steps so his shadow covers Rowan without a word.
6. A volunteer calls Kai back to the sheet; Kai turns on the charm and walks away with the clipboard's pen.

**Dialogue**
```
VOLUNTEER (bright, holding out the sheet): "Kai Santana! You have to sign up for something."
KAI (walking backward, all charm): "I signed up for everything last year. Look where it got me."
ROWAN (low, squinting): "It's ninety degrees."
ELIAS (not looking at him): "Then stand where I stand."
MALIK (to Kai, quiet): "Store tonight?"
KAI (easy, already moving): "Store every night."
```

### 030 PPC — The Paper Cut
Story beat: **Set-Up** (a paper cut turns every head at once, and Mara starts to notice).

**Action**
1. A freshman lecture hall; the professor handing out a syllabus row by row.
2. Two rows up, a student slices a finger on the paper; one bead of red.
3. Kai, Malik, Elias, Rowan and Lucian turn their heads at the same instant, the same angle.
4. Mara Voss, one row behind them, sees all five heads move together.
5. Kai catches himself first and turns back; the others follow a beat late.
6. Mara writes something in the margin of her syllabus.
7. Malachi, at the end of the row, keeps looking at Mara a beat too long.

**Dialogue**
```
STUDENT (wincing, to no one): "Paper cut. Unbelievable."
MARA (under her breath, writing): "Same second. All of them."
LUCIAN (to Kai, barely moving his lips): "She saw."
KAI (eyes front): "She saw a clique."
```

### 040 VHD — Video Haven, Day
Story beat: **Theme Stated** (who counts as family, and what you'll break to keep them).

**Action**
1. Video Haven by day: half the shelves bare, a CLOSING SALE banner nobody believes.
2. Kai's Mother at the register with the ledger open, pencil hovering over a red column.
3. Kai stocks the horror aisle and fronts The Lost Boys on its stand.
4. A customer returns a tape late; Kai's Mother waives the fee before he can argue.
5. Malik comes in the side door and slides a vial into the Lost Boys case.
6. In the security mirror, Kai sees it, and says nothing.
7. Kai's Mother closes the ledger; Kai takes it from her and puts it under the counter.

**Dialogue**
```
CUSTOMER (embarrassed, sliding the tape over): "It's three days late, I know."
KAI'S MOTHER (waving it off): "Family discount."
CUSTOMER (confused): "I'm not family."
KAI'S MOTHER (smiling, tired): "You came back. That's family."
KAI (from the aisle, light): "That's why we're broke."
KAI'S MOTHER (not looking up): "That's why we're still open."
```
