STORY AND TONE

The Courier stops for a drink in the Night Market. The Crew converges to steal the delivery. The Courier treats the ambush as a minor inconvenience, dismantling the Crew while still drinking. The humor comes entirely from composure: no grinning, no posing, no visible exertion. The delivery is the Courier's only priority.
