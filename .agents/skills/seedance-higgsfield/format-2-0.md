Write the SEEDANCE PROMPT in Higgsfield's Seedance 2.0 format: plain prose with numbered shots. No section headings, no Markdown, no bracketed timeline.

First line: the shot structure, exactly "Total: {{SECONDS}}s / N shots / {{ASPECT}}". Then one sentence of global style: genre, the live-action look, film texture, and any atmosphere that runs through every shot ("rain throughout, every shot").
Declarations, one per line: "@Image_1 = <exact full name> — definitive identity lock" for each reference image, and "No image: <exact name> — <one fixed line>" for every other character and place. An audio or video reference gets its one job: "@Audio_1 = <the track> — master track: keep the original audio from @Audio_1 unchanged, precise lip sync", "@Video_1 = <the clip> — motion, camera and timing only".
Shots: each on its own line, starting "Shot 1:", "Shot 2:", and so on. Use two or three shots for 10 to 15 seconds (about 4 to 6 seconds each), never more than five; below about 10 seconds, two shots at most. Inside each shot, in this order: the subject and one action with a clear endpoint, the setting and its light, one camera move (dolly in, truck left, arc shot, push in, pull back wide, handheld follow, crane up), then its sound. Dialogue goes in quotes, short, inside the shot where the speaker is on screen. End each shot on its completed beat, let the next shot open the new one, and vary the framing from shot to shot. Timestamps only as hints inside a shot ("at 5 seconds…"), never as blocks.
For one unbroken take instead, write "single continuous take, no cuts" and no shots.
Last line: "SFX:" the ambience and specific sound effects, "no music" unless music is wanted, and the negatives (no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks, no on-screen text except the title). To keep a colour or object out, count it ("Count of green elements: zero") instead of writing "no green", which plants the word.

Naming rules (the app's prompt linter checks every one of these, and the prompt is fixed until it passes):
- Declare every character and every place before the story uses them, one per line: "@Image_1 = <exact full name> — definitive identity lock" for anything with a reference image, and "No image: <exact name> — <one fixed line>" for everything else (for example "No image: Drexler — the crew boss, heavyset, dark oilskin coat" or "No image: the car deck — the ferry's sealed lower vehicle hold").
- After that, call each of them only by the exact declared name, in full, every time. Never a first name alone, never a pronoun (he, she, they, him, her, his, their, them…), never a stand-in for a person (man, woman, girl, boy, kid, guy, person, people, figure) or a place (room, building, store, shop, interior, storefront, place, location).
- Words inside quotation marks (dialogue, the title) are exempt.
- Never describe a referenced character's face, hair or clothing. The one exception is a storyboard grid used as the reference: a grid doesn't hold a look the way a sheet does, so put the look in that reference's declaration line, after the dash, and nowhere else.
- Never write a banned word, not even as a negative ("no X" still plants it).
- Nothing is black and white.
