# Example: NINE FLOORS (Seedance 2.5, 12 s, 16:9)

Input: a grid of a violet-haired, tattooed young adult in a crimson apartment corridor, phone in hand, door 4B in several panels.

## The Concept

- **Title:** NINE FLOORS
- **Logline:** When Dario Vance climbs home after a night shift, the ninth-floor corridor keeps starting over, and Dario Vance must reach door 4B before the loop closes for good.
- **The twist:** the missing poster on door 4B shows Dario Vance's own face. Dario Vance has been stuck in this corridor far longer than one night.
- **Accent:** sodium yellow #E8D44D, only on the missing poster.
- **Alternate titles:** 4B · LANDING · THE LONG WALK

## The Seedance Prompt

The prompt is everything between the two rules.

---

Create a 12-second, 16:9 cinematic live-action psychological thriller teaser for "NINE FLOORS."

REFERENCE AND CONTINUITY

@Image_1 = Dario Vance — storyboard grid; young adult, violet hair, a stopped-clock tattoo on the neck, black bomber, thin silver chain
No image: the Ninth Floor corridor — a narrow apartment corridor with crimson walls, a worn dark-red runner carpet down the centre, a radiator on the left wall, door 4B halfway along on the right, a fire door at the far end 18 metres away under a stairwell sign
No image: the missing poster — a photocopied missing poster taped to door 4B

Follow the panels of @Image_1 for framing only. Render every shot as full-screen moving footage; the grid itself never appears. The phone stays in Dario Vance's right hand throughout. Dario Vance walks toward the fire door every time, screen left to right.

STORY AND TONE

Dario Vance comes home late and walks the Ninth Floor corridor toward door 4B, but the fire door at the far end opens straight back onto the same corridor. Dread, not panic: Dario Vance never screams or speaks, and the performance stays tired and quiet until the missing poster.

[00:00–00:04] — THE LANDING

Slow push in down the Ninth Floor corridor at eye height as Dario Vance steps out of the stairwell, the phone screen lighting the jaw. The tungsten bulbs buzz.

Hard cut.

Close on Dario Vance's thumb on the phone: the same unanswered call, listed nine times.

Hard cut.

Low on the runner carpet as Dario Vance's boots pass door 4B without a glance. A warped half-time trap pulse starts under a reversed choir.

[00:04–00:09] — THE LOOP

Handheld follow behind Dario Vance pushing through the fire door at the far end.

Hard cut.

Dario Vance steps out of the stairwell onto the Ninth Floor corridor again: the same bulbs, the same radiator, the same runner.

Hard cut.

Close on the stairwell sign above the fire door, a painted "9".

Hard cut.

Dario Vance breaks into a run toward door 4B, the bulbs passing faster overhead. The beat doubles.

[00:09–00:12] — DOOR 4B

Dario Vance pulls up at door 4B mid-stride, still moving, and the camera pushes in on the missing poster: the photocopied face is Dario Vance's own, above the word "MISSING".

The music cuts completely. Only the bulbs buzzing.

Hard cut to black.

Title: "NINE FLOORS"

Silence, then one door latch clicks.

VISUALS AND SOUND

Photoreal live action, shot on ARRI Alexa with anamorphic lenses, 35mm film grain, crimson walls under warm tungsten, deep shadows that still hold texture. The only yellow in the teaser is the missing poster, sodium yellow #E8D44D. Count of other yellow objects: zero. The camera stays at eye height or lower, one move per shot; everything plays at full speed, with no slow motion.

A warped half-time trap pulse with a reversed choir, rising through THE LOOP and cutting out completely on the missing poster. Ambience: buzzing bulbs, the fire door's heavy clunk, footsteps on carpet. No dialogue and no narration. No subtitles. Only the final title appears onscreen.
